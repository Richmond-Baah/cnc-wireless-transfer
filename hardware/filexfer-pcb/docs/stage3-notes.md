# FileXfer PCB — Stage 3 notes (pre-checks + design decisions)

Legend: **[V]** checked in the named datasheet/drawing; **[U]** unverified.

## 0. Decisions from Stage 2 (user, 2026-11-09)
- One PCB and one case length for TX and RX (the case grows). No short TX variant.
- LDL1117S33R. XKB U217 plug is primary, Molex 48037 alternate; the plug is a DNP option so the first 10 can be hand-soldered.
- SD power defaults ON (pull-up on the switch's ON pin, driven by GPIO2).
- LED: WS2812B-2020 powered from VBUS 5 V; data through SN74AHCT1G125 (GPIO48 unchanged).
- Placement: plug → SD socket → module, antenna at the far tip, keep-out clear of plug/SD metal.

## 1. LDL1117 output capacitor / ESR with ceramics [V ST DocID030319 Rev 3 §6.2]
- "The control loop of the LDL1117 is designed to work with an output ceramic capacitor"; X5R/X7R suggested.
- Input ≥ 1 µF, close to VIN. Suggested combination: **1 µF in / 4.7 µF out**.
- The stability plan (min C / ESR region) is only plotted for VOUT = 5 V and 1.2 V (Fig 20/21); **3.3 V is not plotted** [U]. Ceramic 4.7 µF X7R sits inside both plotted regions.
- Design: CIN 4.7 µF (≥ 1 µF min), COUT 4.7 µF X7R 0603/0805 at the pins.

## 2. Plug PCB thickness / height

|---|---|---|
| Specified PCB thickness | **1.60 ± 0.05 mm** (note 3) | not stated |

| Tail plane (= PCB top) from shell top | 3.10 mm → shell **+3.10 / –1.40** about PCB top | 1.90 from one face, 2.60 from the other → **+2.60/–1.90 or +1.90/–2.60** [U which face] |
| Shell legs | 4.80 below shell top = 1.70 below PCB top (0.1 mm through 1.6 mm) | slots, leg length [U] |
| Mounting | overhangs the PCB edge (pads 2.75 mm from the edge) | overhangs the edge (layout) |

- **Board thickness: 1.6 mm** (matches Molex; nothing in the XKB drawing conflicts).
- Off-centre check: a host receptacle mates with the plug's own shell, so PCB thickness does not move the plug relative to the receptacle. What matters is the plug versus the case/board stack:
  - Molex: plug centre +0.85 mm above PCB top; stack envelope –1.6…+3.10 (centre +0.75) → **0.1 mm off-centre**.
  - XKB: plug centre +0.35 or –0.35 mm → **≤ 0.4 mm off-centre** relative to the stack (centre +0.75 → 0.4/1.1 mm). Not significant for mating; the case nose must be centred on the plug, not on the PCB. [U until the XKB 3D model/sample confirms the seating face]
- **XKB and Molex footprints differ** (pad positions/edge distance), so the alternate is not drop-in. Rev A uses the XKB footprint; the Molex needs its own footprint/rev.
- Height budget: max(plug +3.10, module 2.4, SD 2.0) + PCB 1.6 = **4.7 mm** stack against 7.3 mm inner.

## 3. Inrush versus USB 50 µC
Limit: ≤ 10 µF effective across VBUS at attach ↔ 50 µC at 5 V (Microchip, TI ISOUSB211, USB-IF Device Capacitance ECN; secondary sources). The USB-IF test method was not read [U].

Without soft-start (everything charged within µs through the LDO's 1.5–2 A current limit):
Q = 5 V·C_VBUS + 3.3 V·C_3V3 = 5 × 4.9 µF (4.7 LDO-in + 2 × 0.1) + 3.3 × (4.7 LDO-out + 10 module + 22 bulk + 1 + 1 EN + about 0.6 decoupling + SD rail about 4.8) ≈ 24.5 + 145 ≈ **170 µC → fails** (and the module's internal caps add more [U]).

To meet 50 µC without soft-start, C_3V3 ≤ (50 – 24.5)/3.3 ≈ 7.7 µF, which is below Espressif's ≥ 10 µF at the module. **So soft-start is needed.**

Design: a second **TPS22917** (U-SS) between the LDO output (3V3_LDO) and the main rail (+3V3), ON tied to its VIN, CT = 2.2 nF (CT → VIN), QOD open.
- tR at VIN 3.6 V = 1.6 µs/pF → **≈ 3.5 ms**; tON 3.8 µs/pF → about 8.4 ms [V TI SLVSDW8B §7.6].
- Ramp ≈ 0.8·3.3 V / 3.5 ms ≈ 0.75 V/ms → inrush into about 45 µF ≈ **34 mA** (≪ 100 mA unconfigured).
- Fast charge from VBUS = 5 × 4.9 + 3.3 × 4.7 ≈ **40 µC ≤ 50 µC** (nominal values; DC-bias derating only lowers it).
- Cost: +1 × TPS22917 (about $0.29 @100), Ron 80 mΩ → 32 mV at 0.4 A.
- The SD rail sits behind its own TPS22917 (CT 1 nF → tR ≈ 1.6 ms, QOD tied to VOUT for a fast power-cycle discharge) and is fed from +3V3, so it does not draw from VBUS directly.
- The ESP32 EN RC (10 k / 1 µF) is on +3V3 (after soft-start).

## 4. LED from VBUS
- WS2812B-2020 VDD = +5V (VBUS after polyfuse), 100 nF. SN74AHCT1G125: VCC +5V, OE̅ to GND, A = GPIO48 (10 k pull-down so the LED stays dark while the ESP boots), Y → 100 Ω → DIN.
- AHCT VCC range is 4.5–5.5 V; VBUS after the fuse and cable may sag below 4.5 V under Wi-Fi peaks → the buffer runs out of spec (VIH 2.0 V stays met; output high ≈ VCC) [U, bench].
- +5V caps (2 × 100 nF) are counted in C_VBUS above.
